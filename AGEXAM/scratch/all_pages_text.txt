Concept: Matching of objects that share some kind of a relationship 
----
Introduction: Have pairs of objects that go together or can be used together (objects that 
share some kind of relationship) such as bowl and spoon, socks and shoes, pencil and eraser 
or pencil and notebook, paint and brush, and so on. Take out one item, and ask the children 
what can be used with it.

Concept: Grouping of objects according to their common features 
Introduction: Discuss the organization of. the classroom-all the bags are on the shelf or on 
the benches, the chalks in a box, books on the shelf, toys in the basket, and so on. Display 
a set of chalks, crayons, pencils and erasers in a tray, and ask the children to group them 
suitably. Ask the children to form various kinds of groups, according to gender, height,
